# Workshop 3 — Governance & Metrics

**Time:** 90 min · **Output:** decision rights table, steering charter, KPI definitions, ROI convention confirmed

## Objective

Agree who decides what, how the team reports, and how value is measured. Complete the governance skeleton in playbook §4 and the measurement standards in §5.

## Prep (before the session)

- Facilitator: prepare a **draft decision rights table** — for each gate (G1–G5), who Decides / Consults / Informs (D-C-I). Skeleton in playbook §4.
- Prepare a filled sample steering 1-pager (use the template with realistic numbers) so the format discussion is concrete.
- Draft KPI definitions for the standard set: hours saved, cycle time, error rate, adoption, cost per transaction.
- **Invite the sponsor** (for decision rights) and **Finance** (for the ROI convention confirmation).

## Attendees

EPO team + sponsor + Finance.

## Agenda

| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Setup | The three decisions needed today: decision rights, steering cadence/format, KPI definitions + ROI convention |
| 10–35 | Decision rights | Walk each gate. For G1–G5: who Decides, who Consults, who Informs? Resolve the disputed rows. Escalation path: who does an EPO escalate to, and when? |
| 35–55 | Steering & reporting | Cadence: monthly steering 1-pager, quarterly value reviews. Format: one-format rule (the steering template, no bespoke decks). Walk the sample 1-pager and edit it together. |
| 55–80 | Metrics | For each KPI: definition, data source, owner, target. Confirm the ROI convention with Finance: 1 FTE = 2,080 h/yr; fully loaded $36k/yr — adjust if payroll reality differs. |
| 80–90 | Charter sign-off | Read back the agreed decisions; note open items; log everything in the decision log |

## Facilitation tips

- Decision rights will be the most contested 25 minutes — let disputes surface, but timebox: unresolved rows get an owner + date instead of endless debate.
- The Finance representative is there to confirm the ROI convention, not to review every number. Keep them to their lane.
- The one-format rule (no bespoke steering decks) will meet resistance — hold the line; it's the anti-bureaucracy guardrail.

## Outputs & follow-ups

- [ ] Decision rights table (D-C-I per gate) → update playbook §4
- [ ] Steering charter: cadence, format, escalation path → update playbook §4
- [ ] KPI definitions with sources and owners → update playbook §5
- [ ] ROI convention confirmed or adjusted → update ROI model + playbook §5
- [ ] Decision log entries from the session

## Success criteria

- Every gate has a named decision owner.
- Steering cadence, format, and escalation path are agreed.
- Each KPI has a definition, source, and owner.
- ROI convention is confirmed (or explicitly changed) with Finance present.
