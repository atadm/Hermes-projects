# Workshop 1 — Role & Process

**Time:** 90 min · **Output:** adapted process map, gate definitions of done, role boundaries

## Objective

Validate the EPO role and the 6-phase / 5-gate lifecycle against the team's real context. By the end, the team agrees: what an EPO owns, what the phases/gates look like in practice, and what "done" means at each gate.

## Prep (before the session)

- Participants bring **one current project each** (name, current phase, one pain point) — fill the [project mapping worksheet](worksheets/project-mapping-worksheet.md) beforehand.
- Read playbook §1 (Role) and §2 (Process) — 10 minutes.
- Facilitator: print the phase map (6 phases, 5 gates) large enough for the whole group.

## Attendees

EPO team + 1–2 process owners (they ground the discussion in real operations).

## Agenda

| Time | Segment | Activity |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Setup | Why this matters; agenda; the ground rules; how decisions get recorded |
| 10–25 | Role boundaries | Silent brainstorm: "what does an EPO own / NOT own?" → group on a board → dot-vote the top 5 non-ownership items. Challenge: who is accountable if the EPO doesn't own it? |
| 25–55 | Map real projects | Each participant places their project on the phase wall and says where it's stuck. Group looks for patterns: which phases/gates are causing friction? What's missing? |
| 55–80 | Gate definitions of done | Walk G1–G5. For each gate, agree/edit the definition of done as a group. (Draft DoDs are in playbook §2 — edit, don't start from scratch.) |
| 80–90 | Wrap-up | Decisions logged; owners for follow-ups; session success check |

## Facilitation tips

- When mapping projects, look for **where projects die**: usually between Discovery and Delivery, or at Adoption. Name it if the pattern appears.
- If the team has no real projects yet, use two hypothetical but concrete cases (one automation, one transformation).
- The DoD discussion is where the process becomes the team's own — let them rewrite definitions even if it slows the session.

## Outputs & follow-ups

- [ ] Adapted phase/gate map (markup saved to Confluence/Figma/diagrams.net)
- [ ] Agreed definitions of done for all 5 gates → update playbook §2
- [ ] Role non-ownership list → update playbook §1
- [ ] Decision log entries from the session
- [ ] Owners: <who> updates playbook by <date>

## Success criteria

- Every participant mapped ≥1 real project onto the phases.
- Every gate has an agreed definition of done.
- The role boundaries (owns / doesn't own) are agreed — no open disputes left in the room.
