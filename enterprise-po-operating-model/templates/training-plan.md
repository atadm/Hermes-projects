# Training Plan & Materials Checklist — <Project>

**Purpose:** Close the skill gaps identified in the Change Impact Assessment. Training is done **before** go-live, and its completion is a Gate 4 condition.

## Plan

| # | Audience | Current skill | Target skill | Format | Duration | When | Owner | Completion criteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | <e.g. AP clerks> | Manual entry | Exception handling in new workflow | Hands-on workshop + sandbox | 2 h | Launch -1w | EPO + L&D | 100% attended + sandbox task done |
| 2 | <team leads> | Approve in inbox | Approve in tool, manage team dashboards | Role guide + 30-min session | 30 min | Launch -1w | EPO | 100% attended |
| 3 | <support tier 1> | n/a | First-line troubleshooting | Runbook + demo | 1 h | Launch -2w | Delivery | Walkthrough OK'd |
| 4 | <trainers> | n/a | Deliver the above | Train-the-trainer | 2 h | Launch -2w | EPO + L&D | Certification |

## Materials checklist

- [ ] Quick reference cards (1 page per role, screenshots) — owner, date
- [ ] Sandbox / demo environment access for all learners — owner, date
- [ ] E-learning or recorded session for late joiners / remote staff — owner, date
- [ ] FAQ (from Comms Plan) linked in every session
- [ ] Test environment with realistic sample data (no production data in training)
- [ ] "What changed vs what I used to do" cheat sheet per role (bridges old → new)
- [ ] Feedback form per session (what was unclear → FAQ update)
- [ ] Train-the-trainer material where rollout is wave-based

## Success measures

| Measure | Target | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Attendance rate | 100% of impacted users | Training log |
| Post-training task completion | ≥ 90% | Sandbox results |
| Post-training survey (confidence) | ≥ 4/5 | Survey |
| Support tickets in first 2 weeks | ≤ threshold from hypercare plan | Helpdesk |

## Rules

- No go-live without training completion (Gate 4 condition — playbook §2).
- Training is role-specific, not one-size: clerks don't need the admin session and vice versa.
- Refresher at +6 weeks for stragglers; adoption tracker shows who needs it.
