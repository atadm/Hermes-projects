# Decision Log — <Project / Team>

**Purpose:** Record every significant decision so nothing lives in tribal knowledge. Append-only; decisions are superseded, never deleted.

## Rules

1. Record a decision when it is made (gate decisions, scope changes, vendor choices, RAID-triggered calls).
2. Include context and options considered — future readers need the *why*, not just the *what*.
3. Status: `Open` → `Decided` → `Implemented` → `Superseded by #<n>`.
4. One line per decision; link to related artifacts (business case, PRD, RAID item).
