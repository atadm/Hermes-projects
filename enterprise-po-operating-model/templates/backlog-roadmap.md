## Rules (non-negotiable)

1. **Single backlog** per product — no private lists on desks or in chats.
2. **Priority comes from the business case**, not from who shouts loudest. Every P0 item must trace to a business case benefit.
3. **No scope creep without a decision log entry** — new requirements go through the PRD change process and a version bump.
4. **Definition of done** per item: code/config done + tested + demoed + accepted by process owner.
5. **Sprint cadence + demo cadence** fixed from the start (e.g. 2-week sprints, demo every sprint).
6. Roadmap is reviewed at steering; backlog is re-prioritized at every sprint planning.

## Sprint health (what steering sees)

- Velocity trend (stable or declining = signal)
- % of sprint items accepted vs carried over
- Open blockers (link to RAID log)
- Demo attendance by process owner
