# Comms Plan — <Project>

**Purpose:** Who hears what, when, through which channel. Built from the Stakeholder Map and Change Impact Assessment — never from scratch.

## Principles

1. **3 messages max** per audience — anything more is noise.
2. **Sponsor speaks first** for significant changes; EPO handles the operational cadence.
3. Answer **"what's in it for me"** explicitly for impacted users.
4. Two-way: every channel has a way to ask questions (FAQ, Q&A session, feedback link).
5. No surprises: impacted groups hear about changes **before** the general announcement.

## Audience × message × channel

| # | Audience | Key message (max 3) | Channel | Frequency / timing | Owner | Success measure |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sponsor / steering | Progress, decisions needed | Steering 1-pager | Monthly | EPO | Decisions taken |
| 2 | Process owner | Milestones, what we need from them | 1:1 + working session | Weekly | EPO | Attendance, input quality |
| 3 | Impacted users | What changes, when, what's in it for me, how to get help | Team meeting + email + FAQ page | Launch -2w, launch, +2w | EPO + Comms | Questions received, sentiment |
| 4 | Adjacent teams | What's changing, where to look | Newsletter / wiki update | At launch | Comms | No surprise questions |
| 5 | IT / support | Operational handover | Technical update + runbook | Before Gate 4 | EPO + delivery | Support readiness |

## Phasing

| Phase | Timing | Focus | Key activities |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-launch (awareness) | T-4w → T-0 | Why, what, when | Sponsor announcement, FAQ, demo invites |
| Launch (activation) | Go-live ± 2w | How to use, where to get help | Training done, hypercare comms, feedback channels open |
| Post-launch (reinforce) | +2w → +3m | Adoption, wins, refresher | Success stories, adoption updates, refresher tips, retirement of old way |
