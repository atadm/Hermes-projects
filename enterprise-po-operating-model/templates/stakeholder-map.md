# Stakeholder Map — <Project>

**Purpose:** Identify every stakeholder and choose the right engagement strategy. Build at Phase 1 (Discovery), revisit at every phase — stakeholders change as the project moves.

## Power–interest grid

Place each stakeholder in a quadrant. Strategy is determined by the quadrant, not by personal preference.

```
                 HIGH POWER
                      |
      KEEP SATISFIED  |  MANAGE CLOSELY
      (consult,       |  (active engagement,
       keep happy)    |   co-decide)
                      |
   LOW INTEREST ------+------ HIGH INTEREST
                      |
      MONITOR         |  KEEP INFORMED
      (minimal        |  (regular updates,
       effort)        |   listen to concerns)
                      |
                 LOW POWER
```

| Quadrant | Strategy |
|---|---|
| Manage closely | Co-decide, frequent 1:1s, early involvement, include in steering |
| Keep satisfied | Consult on big decisions, manage expectations, protect from surprises |
| Keep informed | Regular updates via comms plan, feedback channels |
| Monitor | Occasional updates only — do not over-engage |

## How to use

- One row per stakeholder or per role-group (don't map 300 employees — map the groups).
- Revisit at each gate; a stakeholder moving quadrants is a signal, not a problem.
- Feeding: engagement strategy → Comms Plan · resistance signals → Change Impact Assessment.
