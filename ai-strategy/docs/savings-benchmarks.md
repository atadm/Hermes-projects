# Savings Benchmarks by Department — B2B SaaS (Aug 2026)

Status: external benchmarks, not company data. Central values are conservative-to-moderate
modeling picks for a mid-size B2B SaaS company (250-1,000 FTE). Label as assumptions when used.

## Department savings benchmarks (% of department spend)

| Department | Savings range | Central (model) | Key sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer Support / Success | 20-35% net, yr 1 | 30% | Fin AI: SaaS/tech avg 39% cost reduction; Zendesk median T1 deflection 41.2%; QJE field study 15% productivity lift (36% for low-skilled); Intercom ~$3.50 ROI |
| R&D / Software Engineering | 15-30% lift | 20% | McKinsey 20-45% of function spend; GitHub 26%; arXiv 55.8% (controlled); Cui 2025 26.08% more tasks/week |
| Sales | 5-20% | 12% | McKinsey 3-5% (spend); Lumen 4h/seller/wk + $50M/yr; Softcat 20% admin reduction; BDR 2-3x outbound throughput (vendor) |
| Marketing | 8-15% | 12% | McKinsey 8-15% of marketing spend; content gen ~40% faster (writing benchmark) |
| G&A — Finance | 15-40% | 25% | Deloitte ~40% analyst hrs on routine reports; IBM Finance >90% cycle-time on close; Forrester 275% ROI |
| G&A — HR | 15-40% | 25% | IBM AskHR 40% HR opex reduction, 94% containment, 75% ticket reduction; ServiceNow 410k hrs/yr saved |
| G&A — Legal | 25-50% review-cycle (bounded) | 25% | BCG/HBS 25.1% faster knowledge work; vendor cases (low-med confidence) |
| G&A — IT | 30-55% | 30% | TELUS 55% infra cost reduction; Forethought up to 80% cloud cost reduction |

## Weighted company-wide model (labor-heavy depts)

Combines SaaS Capital 2026 cost structure (% of ARR) with central savings above.

| Dept | Cost (% ARR) | Savings % | → % ARR |
|---|---|---|---|
| R&D / Eng | 22% | 20% | 4.4% |
| Sales | 15% | 12% | 1.8% |
| G&A (Fin/Legal/HR/IT) | 15% | 25% | 3.8% |
| Support + Success | 9% | 30% | 2.7% |
| Marketing | 8% | 12% | 1.0% |
| **Total (labor depts)** | **69%** | | **13.6% of ARR** (range 8.7-20%) |

Cross-check: HBR reports avg ~15.7% cost savings from genAI adoption — consistent with the
13.6% weighted estimate.

## Caveats
- These are gross productivity/cost-reduction potentials, not realized cash savings. Actual
  realized savings depend on adoption, redeployment (vs headcount reduction), and net of AI
  licensing/infra costs.
- Support is the fastest/most measurable payback (3-6 mo); R&D and G&A are the biggest pools
  but slower and harder to convert to cash.
- Vendor case figures are self-reported; the QJE/GitHub/Cui/BCG studies are the strongest evidence.
