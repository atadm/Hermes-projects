# AI Strategy

_Company-wide AI strategy for a mid-size SaaS company (250-1,000 employees)._

## Status

- **State:** WS1 in progress (external research seeded; awaiting company data)
- **Primary objective:** Cost reduction / operational efficiency
- **Target:** Draft strategy within weeks (~4 weeks)
- **Last updated:** 2026-08-26

## Overview

The company is cloud-native with a mature data platform and analytics/BI in place.
The strategy focuses on applying AI to reduce cost and drive operational efficiency
across the business, then scales to product value where quick wins exist.
